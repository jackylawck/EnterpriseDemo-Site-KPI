📌 專案摘要與架構定位 📌 Executive Summary & Architectural Positioning

本儲存庫為企業管治與工程現場數位轉型之開源概念驗證（PoC），展示如何以「治理即代碼」（Governance-as-Code）原則重塑建造業地盤績效考核。

This repository is an open-source Enterprise Demo illustrating how Governance-as-Code modernizes construction site performance appraisals.

本系統將傳統紙本《高級管工關鍵績效指標（KPI）考核協議書》全面數位化，採用 Streamlit 行動優先介面與純 Python ReportLab 報表核心。系統於底層落實無資料庫零資料保留（Zero-Data-Retention）架構，僅在單次會話記憶體中即時計算與渲染報告，兼顧香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（PDPO 第 486 章）資料最小化原則與 ISO 9001:2015 條款 7.5 文件可追溯性。

Built on a mobile-first Streamlit interface and pure-Python ReportLab engine, the platform digitizes traditional paper-based Senior Foreman KPI agreements. Operating strictly on a stateless Zero-Data-Retention architecture with no persistent database, it aligns with Hong Kong PDPO (Cap. 486) data minimization principles and ISO 9001:2015 Clause 7.5 documented audit trails.

在演算法治理層面，全數 11 項 KPI 評分門檻與重大違規一票否決規則均為公開透明之確定性邏輯（Deterministic Logic），並強制保留人類監督（Human-in-the-Loop）四方簽署程序，主動對齊 ISO/IEC 42001 透明度要求並排除黑箱 AI 自動化決策風險。

All 11 KPI thresholds and major-breach veto rules rely solely on transparent deterministic logic—avoiding black-box AI scoring while aligning with ISO/IEC 42001 explainability principles and enforcing mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) 4-party sign-off.

🏛️ 流程生命週期與四階治理閉環 🏛️ Four-Stage Governance Lifecycle

針對傳統地盤考核「紙本難攜、人工手算易錯、扣分口說無憑、審計鏈斷裂」四大痛點，系統建立標準化四階閉環：

Designed to eliminate paper friction, manual calculation errors, unverified deductions, and broken audit trails across construction sites:

階段 01 • 現場量測Stage 01 • On-Site Input
行動端實時動態算分Mobile Real-Time Scoring

主管單手輸入原始次數與成本，即時運算比率與 11 項 KPI 得分，零表單提交延遲。Supervisors enter raw counts and cost figures on mobile for instant percentage and 11-KPI scoring.

階段 02 • 實據追溯Stage 02 • Traceability
ISO 7.5 證據鏈綁定ISO 7.5 Evidence Binding

每份評核紀錄綁定地盤日誌、驗收批次單或安全巡查編號，確保扣分客觀可核實。Binds site logs, inspection batch IDs, or safety audit references to eliminate unsubstantiated deductions.

階段 03 • 風險管控Stage 03 • Compliance Gate
一票否決與 CAPA/PIP 連動Veto & CAPA/PIP Trigger

重大失聯或安全違規強制判定 F 級，自動提示啟動 ISO 糾正措施（CAPA）與改善計劃（PIP）。Major breaches force Grade F and automatically flag ISO CAPA or 30–60 day PIP procedures.

階段 04 • 存證歸檔Stage 04 • Audit Closure
ReportLab 四方簽署 PDF4-Party Sign-Off PDF

匯出標準檔名（[編號]_[姓名]_[日期].pdf）官方 A4 報告，會話結束即清除伺服器記憶體。Exports standardized [ID]_[Name]_[Date].pdf A4 reports and purges session memory immediately.

          ⚙️ 技術亮點與合規工程實踐
          ⚙️ Technical & Compliance Engineering Highlights
        

        

          

            
1. 零資料保留架構Zero-Data-Retention Architecture

            
全系統採無狀態記憶體計算，不設後端永久資料庫，用畢即棄，從架構層面杜絕員工績效個資外洩風險。Stateless in-memory computation with zero persistent database eliminates backend PII exposure risks by design.

          

          

            
2. 確定性透明演算法Deterministic Scoring Transparency

            
完整開源 11 項 KPI 算分規則與閥值條件，無黑箱 AI 推論，對齊 ISO/IEC 42001 可解釋性與審計要求。Open-source thresholds for all 11 KPIs without black-box AI, aligning with ISO/IEC 42001 explainability and auditability.

          

          

            
3. 純 Python ReportLab 引擎Pure-Python ReportLab PDF Engine

            
捨棄依賴 C 語言底層庫之方案，以純 Python 生成矢量 A4 四方簽署報表，確保雲端部署 100% 穩定相容。Eliminates C-library deployment dependencies, generating vector A4 4-party sign-off reports reliably across cloud platforms.

          

          

            
4. ISO 7.5 實據與四方閉環ISO 7.5 Evidence & 4-Party Closure

            
強制串聯工程日誌單號與被考核者、主管、QMS/HR 審核員及公司代表四方簽署，兼顧程序正義與稽核留痕。Pairs site log references with 4-party sign-off (Employee, Supervisor, QMS/HR Auditor, Authorized Rep) for due process.
